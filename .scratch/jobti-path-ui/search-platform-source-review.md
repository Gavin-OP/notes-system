# Search platform source review

Reviewed: 2026-08-14

## Mainland China job boards

- [BOSS Zhipin](https://www.zhipin.com/) — general job search, company listings, and campus entry point.
- [51job](https://www.51job.com/) — general recruitment and job search.
- [Zhaopin](https://www.zhaopin.com/) — general recruitment and job search.
- [Shixiseng](https://www.shixiseng.com/) — internship-focused entry point.
- [Nowcoder Jobs](https://www.nowcoder.com/jobs/) — campus and technology recruitment entry point.

## Hong Kong job boards

- [Jobsdb Hong Kong](https://hk.jobsdb.com/) — broad role and company search with saved searches and application history.
- [CTgoodjobs](https://www.ctgoodjobs.hk/) — broad Hong Kong job search and career resources.
- [cpjobs](https://www.cpjobs.com/hk/en) — Hong Kong job search.
- [Hong Kong Civil Service Bureau recruitment](https://www.csb.gov.hk/english/recruit/7.html) — official civil-service recruitment entry point.

## Social and community sources

- [LinkedIn Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/) and public profiles — role announcements, employee backgrounds, and company updates.
- [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/) — job listings, company reviews, and interview/salary contributions.
- [Xiaohongshu](https://www.xiaohongshu.com/) — experience posts and peer discussion; claims require independent verification.

## Verification rule

Treat dates, eligibility, location, responsibilities, and application status as unverified until confirmed on an employer's official careers site or in direct recruiter communication. Use multiple independent posts to identify patterns, not a single post to establish a fact.
