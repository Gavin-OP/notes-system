# Company Career Pages

An employer's careers site is usually the clearest source for role status, programme details, and application requirements. It may also reveal team categories, events, and candidate FAQs that job boards omit.

## Keep a light watchlist

Record companies you genuinely care about, their careers links, and available alerts. Watching relevant opening windows is enough; you do not need to patrol every page daily.

## Check site-specific details

Review the process, location constraints, materials, and timeline. Use the official page as the final reference and retain the listing or confirmation email.
