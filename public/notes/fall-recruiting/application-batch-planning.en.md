# Application Batch Planning

Batching applications can steadily increase your chances of being seen without turning volume into another KPI. Grouping similar roles reduces repeated preparation and protects time for opportunities you value most.

## Batch by similarity

Group roles by responsibilities, materials, and deadlines. Reuse a sound base, then adjust what matters. Clearly unsuitable roles do not need to become numbers on a tracker.

## Leave capacity for later stages

Applications may soon become tests, calls, and interviews. Leave space to handle them, then adjust the next batch using response rates and your experience.
