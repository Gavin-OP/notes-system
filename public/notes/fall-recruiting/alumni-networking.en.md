# Alumni Networking

Alumni can offer context about roles, teams, locations, and early-career transitions. Reach out with a short introduction, a genuine reason for contacting them, and a small request such as a brief conversation.

Prepare questions that cannot be answered by a company page, respect their time, and send thanks afterwards. Treat the conversation as learning and relationship building rather than an automatic route to a referral.
