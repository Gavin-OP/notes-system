# Campus Recruiting

Campus recruiting is a focused approach where employers, universities, and students connect during specific hiring periods. It goes beyond online applications to include information sessions, on-campus interviews, university-exclusive job links, career fairs, and alumni events.

## Use University Channels

Your university is a key source for campus recruiting information. Explore your school's career platform, department messages, career center, and student societies. Many companies post opportunities exclusively for your institution's students or tailor programs to specific majors and graduation dates. Always confirm eligibility criteria and application deadlines with official sources.

## Treat Events as Information Sources

Attending campus recruiting events, whether online talks or in-person fairs, is a valuable opportunity to learn about companies and roles. Research participating companies beforehand and prepare thoughtful questions, such as:

*   What does a typical day look like in this role?
*   What's the team culture like?
*   What training and development opportunities are available?
*   What does the selection process involve?

Even without an immediate offer, understanding role names, recruitment timelines, and key contacts can significantly inform your future applications. Record valuable insights and contact details as you go.

## Understand Recruitment Timelines

Campus recruitment timelines vary significantly by industry, employer, school, and country. Some large companies or popular programs (like summer internships or graduate schemes) may open applications a year or more before graduation, while others recruit on a rolling basis or closer to the graduation date. Monitor the recruitment calendars of your target companies and university to avoid missing key deadlines.

## Prepare for Engagement

Being well-prepared for campus recruiting activities helps you make a strong impression:

*   **Concise Self-Introduction:** Prepare a 30-second "elevator pitch" that clearly states who you are, your interests, and your goals.
*   **Updated Resume/Profile:** Keep your resume or online profile (e.g., LinkedIn) current and tailored to your target roles.
*   **Thoughtful Questions:** Prepare researched questions that demonstrate genuine interest and critical thinking about the company and role.
*   **Recording Tools:** Carry a notebook or use a mobile app to jot down conversation points, contact names, and next steps.
