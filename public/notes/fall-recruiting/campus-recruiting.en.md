# Campus Recruiting

Campus recruiting concentrates employers, universities, and students into shared hiring windows. It can include applications, talks, campus interviews, university-only links, and alumni events.

## Use university channels

Check your careers platform, department messages, career centre, and societies. Some opportunities have school or graduation-date rules, so rely on official eligibility and dates.

## Treat events as information sources

Learn who is attending and bring questions that affect your decision. Even without an immediate opportunity, role names, timelines, and contacts can make the next application more informed.
