# Assessment Centre

An Assessment Centre may combine group work, cases, presentations, role play, written exercises, and interviews over several hours. It is a collection of work simulations, not one endless exam.

Confirm the schedule, location, dress guidance, equipment, online platform, and breaks. Different exercises may have different assessors, so one awkward moment does not determine the entire day.

For cases, read the deliverable and time limit before the detail. Separate facts, assumptions, recommendations, and risks. A presentation can use a simple structure: problem, judgement, evidence, recommendation, risks, and next step.

In group work, help the team progress rather than performing a title. In role play, understand the other person’s goal before proposing a solution. Prepare water, food, medication, and any accessibility arrangements you need. Between exercises, take a short note and reset for the next task.
