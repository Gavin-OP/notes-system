# Preparing for Online Tests

An online assessment may include numerical or verbal reasoning, logical patterns, situational judgement, work-style questions, coding tasks, or a recorded video response. Read the invitation carefully so you know the format, deadline, time limit, equipment, and practice resources.

Use official practice material first. Practise the interface and pacing as well as the question type. For judgement tests, consider the role, customer, risk, teamwork, and escalation rather than trying to reverse-engineer a perfect personality.

Choose a stable connection and quiet environment, close unnecessary applications, and check accessibility arrangements in advance if you need them. Technical trouble should be documented and reported promptly through the stated support channel.
