# Is HKICPA QP Right for Me?

HKICPA's Qualification Programme is part of the route towards qualification as a certified public accountant in Hong Kong. It is most directly connected to accounting, audit, assurance, reporting, and related professional work.

The route involves academic eligibility, programme modules and assessments, and relevant practical experience. Exact entry routes differ, so check your accredited study, conversion needs, current fees, examination schedule, and experience requirements directly with HKICPA.

For a general finance or technology role, another form of evidence may matter more. Add this branch when accountancy is a serious direction and you want to understand the formal route before committing.
