# Is the HKICPA QP Right for Me?

The HKICPA Qualification Programme (QP) is a professional education, examination, and practical experience pathway to becoming a Hong Kong CPA, closely aligned with Hong Kong auditing, accounting, financial reporting, and related professional services. It is not a standalone certificate exam, but rather a qualification route that requires understanding academic assessment, modules, and work experience.

## First, Understand the Full Structure

According to HKICPA's official explanation, the QP comprises three progressive levels: Associate Level, Professional Level, and Capstone. The full structure includes Associate Level modules, Professional Level modules, and Capstone, combined with workshops, examinations, and practical experience.

Different academic qualifications and course accreditations may lead to different module exemptions. The path may vary for accounting majors, non-accounting majors, and those with Hong Kong versus non-Hong Kong qualifications. Therefore, the first step is to check your eligibility and exemptions, rather than directly choosing an exam.

## Which Roles Have a More Direct Connection?

- Hong Kong Audit / Assurance;
- Accounting, Financial Reporting, and related Advisory;
- Professional service roles with a clear development path towards obtaining the Hong Kong CPA qualification;
- Employers who explicitly offer QP training, or support as an Authorized Employer or Authorized Supervisor.

If your primary goal is investment research, market risk, product, or general data roles, the direct relevance of the QP to your current work might be weaker. When a job description explicitly mentions a professional qualification path, then carefully review the training, exam leave, and fee support provided by the employer.

## Eligibility Assessment and Module Exemptions

First, confirm whether your school and program are on the HKICPA accredited programme list, then check your eligibility assessment results. Official regulations may change with the accreditation year; the same program name does not guarantee the same exemptions.

Applying, registering, accepting assessment results, and enrolling in modules are different steps. Missing the student application period might affect enrolment for the next session, so it's important to view application periods and exam periods separately.

## Practical Experience is Also Part of the Qualification

The HKICPA's Practical Experience Framework requires gaining relevant experience under an approved Authorized Employer or Authorized Supervisor. When looking for a job, you can ask employers if they provide approved supervision, rotations, training, and record-keeping support.

This is why "what role to enter" and "which modules to take" need to be planned together: the work environment will influence your qualification path, and your qualification studies will also affect your pace after joining.

## Turn Accounting Knowledge into Job Application Evidence

- Use public annual reports to explain revenue, costs, cash flow, and key judgments;
- Compare the impact of changes in accounting policies on financial statements;
- Develop an audit risk or internal control case study;
- Translate technical accounting issues into explanations that business professionals can understand;
- Practice identifying problems and proposing necessary evidence with limited information.

The QP emphasizes not only technical knowledge but also analysis, judgment, communication, and professional ethics. These abilities can be demonstrated through case studies and interview answers even before formally completing the qualification.

Check the [HKICPA QP Official Structure](https://www.hkicpa.org.hk/en/Become-a-Hong-Kong-CPA/Qualification-Programme), [Application and Registration](https://www.hkicpa.org.hk/en/Become-a-Hong-Kong-CPA/Qualification-Programme/Application-and-registration), and the [Latest Timetable](https://www.hkicpa.org.hk/en/Become-a-Hong-Kong-CPA/Qualification-Programme/Timetable), then make your decision based on your academic assessment and employer support.
