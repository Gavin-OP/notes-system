# Personal Website

A personal website is a useful home for material that is valuable but too detailed for a resume. It can connect your introduction, selected work, contact details, and downloadable documents in one place.

Keep the structure simple: a short introduction, selected projects or writing, an About section, and a clear contact route. Use readable typography, descriptive links, alt text, and responsive layouts. Check performance and remove anything that distracts from the evidence.

You do not need to code it from scratch. A reliable template or no-code builder is enough unless building the site itself demonstrates a skill relevant to your target roles.
