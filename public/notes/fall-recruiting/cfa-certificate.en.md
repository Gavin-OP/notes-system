# Is the CFA Right for Me?

The CFA Program covers ethics, quantitative methods, economics, financial statement analysis, corporate issuers, equity, fixed income, derivatives, alternatives, and portfolio management.

It is most relevant when these topics appear in the work you want, especially investment research, asset management, portfolio analysis, and some wealth or finance roles. It is less persuasive when the role values a different technical or professional route.

Check the current curriculum, eligibility, fees, exam windows, practical-skills modules, and charter requirements on the official CFA Institute website. Compare that commitment with what would help your applications now: role research, experience, interviews, or a smaller knowledge gap.
