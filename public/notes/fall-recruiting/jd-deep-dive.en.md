# JD Deep Dive

A JD is a public description that may mix essential work, ideal qualifications, and template language. The aim is not to calculate a perfect match score; it is to understand the work and identify credible evidence.

## Separate responsibilities from qualifications

Start with the problems the role handles, then examine tools, experience, soft skills, and credentials. Repeated points tied directly to the work usually deserve more attention.

## Find evidence that can survive follow-up questions

Connect each priority requirement with a real example and anticipate what an interviewer might ask next. For gaps, decide whether to build evidence quickly, explain them honestly, or leave them alone.
