# Offer Decisions and Job-search Review

An offer can bring excitement and a new set of questions. Put the written facts beside your own priorities so the decision becomes easier to examine.

Confirm the role, team, location, start date, probation, base pay, bonus rules, benefits, leave, hours, training or rotations, acceptance deadline, and conditions. Ask HR about unclear terms and keep important answers in writing. Consider tax, living costs, commute, and likely working time rather than comparing one headline number.

## Employment rights and statutory benefits

Use official sources to understand the framework; this overview is not legal advice. Rates, eligibility, and local implementation can change, so check the latest information for the place where you will work.

### Mainland China

- [Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security: Labour Law](https://www.mohrss.gov.cn/xxgk2020/fdzdgknr/zcfg/fl/202011/t20201102_394625.html) covers the basic framework for contracts, working time, rest, wages, and protection.
- [Social Insurance Law](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/bgt/art/2023/art_e81d115419b4463ebb59ec46467fb136.html) introduces the statutory pension, medical, work-injury, unemployment, and maternity systems.
- [Ministry of Justice: Housing Provident Fund Regulations](https://xzfy.moj.gov.cn/c/2021-01-17/488809.shtml) explains the framework for contributions, ownership, withdrawal, and use.

Ask HR when the employment contract will be signed, how probation works, where social insurance and the housing fund will be paid, and how bonuses, overtime, and leave operate. For a specific dispute, consult the local human-resources authority or public legal service.

### Hong Kong

- [Labour Department: A Concise Guide to the Employment Ordinance](https://www.labour.gov.hk/eng/public/ConciseGuide.htm) covers contracts, wages, rest days, leave, termination, and employment protection.
- [MPFA information for employees](https://www.mpfa.org.hk/en/role-based/employees) explains MPF enrolment, contributions, accounts, and what happens when employment ends.

Internships, part-time work, short contracts, and employment-visa situations may be treated differently. Keep written documents and ask the Labour Department, MPFA, or an appropriately qualified adviser when a term is unclear.

Compare the work itself, manager and team, learning, progression, mobility, stability, compensation, location, and daily life. Weight them according to what matters to you now. If declining, respond promptly and politely.

Finally, review the whole search: which channels produced relevant roles, which stories worked, what feedback repeated, and what you learned about the environments where you do your best. An offer is one entry into a career, not a final ranking of your worth.
