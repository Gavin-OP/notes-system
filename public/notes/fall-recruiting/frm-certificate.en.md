# Is the FRM Right for Me?

The FRM curriculum centres on risk foundations, quantitative analysis, financial markets and products, valuation, and market, credit, operational, liquidity, and investment risk.

It can be relevant to risk management, market or credit risk, treasury, risk analytics, and some regulatory or consulting work. Its value depends on whether those capabilities appear in the role and market you are targeting.

Use GARP's official pages for the latest exam dates, fees, curriculum, identification rules, and professional-experience requirements. Before enrolling, compare the preparation period with more immediate evidence you could build through coursework, projects, or practical risk analysis.
