# Portfolio and Personal Website

A portfolio explains selected work in depth. A personal website is the place that organises your introduction, projects, writing, and contact details. They can work together, but they are different tools.

Use either one when you have useful evidence that does not fit on the resume. Keep navigation simple, make your contribution clear, protect confidential information, and test every link before sharing.
