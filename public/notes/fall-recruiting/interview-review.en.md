# Interview Review

Soon after an interview, note the questions, your main points, moments that felt strong, and places where you lost clarity. Separate what you can improve from what was outside your control.

Look for patterns across several interviews: weak company research, stories without clear personal contribution, rushed structure, missing technical knowledge, or an uncertain motivation. Turn only recurring signals into preparation changes.

Not every rejection contains a hidden lesson. Headcount, timing, competition, internal candidates, and team needs also matter. A useful review helps the next conversation; it does not put the entire result on your shoulders.
