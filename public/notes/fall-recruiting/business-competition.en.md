# Business Competitions: Turn Collaboration into Evidence

Business competitions can help you build evidence of commercial analysis, problem framing, and teamwork. A prize is useful, but the stronger story is often how you defined the problem, what you owned, which evidence you used, and how the proposal developed.

## What to look for

- A problem connected to an industry or role you want to explore.
- A time commitment that fits your current job-search pace.
- A clear area of ownership you can discuss in an interview.
- Transparent rules, judging criteria, and terms for using submitted work.

## What to keep

Save the research process, key assumptions, your contribution, and the final presentation. Even without a prize, these can become honest evidence for your resume, portfolio, and behavioral interviews.
