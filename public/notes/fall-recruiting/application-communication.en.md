# Applications and Process Tracking

Before submitting, confirm the role, location, documents, deadline, and contact details. Save the JD because it may disappear before the interview.

Track submission date, current stage, next action, and any promised response date. For email, use a clear subject line, address the recipient correctly, state the purpose early, and keep attachments named professionally. A polite follow-up is reasonable when an agreed timeline has passed.

Recruitment systems are imperfect. Keep confirmation emails and screenshots for important submissions, but allow for delays. A quiet inbox does not automatically say anything about your ability.
