# Career Fairs

A career fair lets you compare employers, practise introducing yourself, and hear information beyond a careers page. You do not need to speak to everyone; a few useful conversations are enough.

## Filter before attending

Choose priority employers while leaving room for one or two surprises. Prepare a short introduction, specific questions, and an easy way to share your resume or LinkedIn.

## Connect the information afterwards

Record roles, timelines, and useful advice, then complete relevant applications. If someone shared contact details, mention where you met and one thing you learned.
