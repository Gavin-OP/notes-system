# Kaggle: Practise a Complete Data Problem

Kaggle is not only for people chasing leaderboard positions. For a beginner, it offers a structured practice space with data, a target, and an evaluation method. You can work through problem understanding, cleaning, a baseline, iteration, and explanation.

## Build something you can show

Choose a manageable dataset and problem. Start with a simple baseline, then record why you changed features, models, or validation. Code is only part of the final story: your interpretation, decisions, and limitations matter too.

## Leave more than a score

A leaderboard score cannot explain your process. A short README covering the problem, method, result, limitations, and next step makes the work much easier for a recruiter to understand.
