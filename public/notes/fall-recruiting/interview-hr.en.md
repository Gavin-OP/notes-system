# HR Interview

An HR interview often checks whether the role, the application, and the person in the conversation tell a coherent story. The questions may sound simple, but specific answers make your choices easier to understand.

## What may come up

- Why this company, industry, and role;
- your experience, strengths, working style, and direction;
- availability, location, and current application status;
- compensation expectations and practical arrangements;
- gaps or changes that need clarification.

## Connect your motivation

Move from your understanding of the role, to evidence you can bring, to why you want to keep doing this work. Company values can provide context, but your answer should contain your own experiences and judgement. You do not need to claim that every role is destiny; describe the work, problems, culture, or growth that genuinely interests you.

## Compensation and logistics

Before naming a range, clarify the level, responsibilities, location, bonus, and overall package. If you are unsure, say you would like to understand the complete role first. Be accurate about start dates and other constraints rather than making promises you cannot keep.

Prepare a short introduction, relevant examples, your reasons for applying, availability, and questions about the process, team, training, and success measures.
