# Preparing Your Resume and Profile

Your resume, LinkedIn, cover letter, portfolio, and personal website do different jobs. Together, they help someone understand your experience quickly and give them somewhere to explore further.

The resume is the concise core. LinkedIn adds context and discoverability. A cover letter explains motivation when the application genuinely calls for it. A portfolio shows work that benefits from visuals or a fuller process. A personal website brings selected material into one place.

You do not need every format. Start with what the roles actually request, then add a branch only when it helps communicate evidence that would otherwise be hard to see.
