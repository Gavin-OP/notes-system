# HR Screening Calls

An HR screening call is often the first live conversation in a hiring process. The recruiter checks broad fit, motivation, communication, timing, and practical conditions before deciding on the next stage.

## Prepare the basic facts clearly

Know your experience, target role, and reason for applying. Be ready to answer honestly about availability, location, work eligibility, and compensation when relevant. Concise answers are enough.

## Treat it as a two-way check

Listen for the process, team, timeline, and next step, and ask about facts that affect your decision. Record what the recruiter emphasised so later preparation can respond to it.
