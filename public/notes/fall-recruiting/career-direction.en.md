# Clarifying Direction and Practical Constraints

Direction comes from the overlap between work you are willing to do, evidence you already have, opportunities available in the market, and gaps you can realistically close.

Use role and market research to test assumptions. Practical constraints such as location, timing, language, and personal priorities belong in the decision too. The aim is a workable next direction, not a permanent answer for the rest of your career.
