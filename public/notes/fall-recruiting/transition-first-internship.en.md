# Starting an Internship After Changing Field

Changing discipline does not mean starting from zero. Your previous work has already built transferable skills such as research, analysis, writing, client communication, delivery, or domain knowledge. The task is to translate that evidence into language the target role recognises.

## Build a bridge, not an entire new degree

Use real JDs to identify recurring foundations, then choose a course, project, or practical task that creates visible evidence quickly. Show that you have begun doing the work, not only that you find it interesting.

## Explain the transition clearly

A credible story covers what drew you to the field, how you tested the interest, what your previous background adds, and what you are currently learning.

## Find entry points open to varied backgrounds

Look for roles mentioning transferable skills, generalist work, rotations, training, or multidisciplinary teams. Conversations and small projects can help distinguish genuine requirements from an idealised wish list.
