# Coffee Chats and Networking

A coffee chat is a short conversation for learning about someone's work and perspective. It is most useful when approached with curiosity rather than as a disguised request for a job.

Ask about the team's work, what a normal week looks like, skills that matter in practice, surprises about the role, early-career learning, and advice they would give someone exploring the field. Questions become stronger when they build on something specific in the person's background.

Introduce yourself briefly, respect the agreed time, listen closely, and leave room for follow-up questions. Avoid asking for confidential interview questions or information that is already easy to find online. Send a concise thank-you message afterwards and mention one useful thing you learned.

Networking can happen through alumni, former colleagues, events, communities, and thoughtful online conversations. A few genuine relationships are more valuable than collecting connections you never speak to again.
