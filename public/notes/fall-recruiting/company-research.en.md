# Company Research

Company research helps you decide whether an opportunity deserves time and makes your reasons for applying more specific. Official sites, reports, products, careers pages, and recent public information are generally stronger than recycled slogans.

## Answer the central questions

What problem does the company solve? Where does the team sit? What would the role work on? Why would you invest in it? Which practical conditions still need checking?

## Connect the information to yourself

Use details that genuinely relate to your experience, interests, or working style. You do not need to understand the entire company; enough evidence to support the decision and conversation is sufficient.
