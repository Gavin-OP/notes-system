# Job Boards

Job boards are useful for scanning the market, learning alternative role titles, and keeping opportunities moving through saved searches and alerts. Update speeds vary, so treat each listing as an entry point rather than the only source of truth.

## Search flexibly

Combine role, skill, industry, and location terms, including synonymous job titles. A few useful saved searches are calmer than restarting from zero every day.

## Verify before investing time

Check the company site for responsibilities, location, deadlines, and application status. Duplicate or vague listings deserve judgment, not extra anxiety.
