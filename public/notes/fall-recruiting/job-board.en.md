# Job Boards

Job boards are useful for scanning the market, learning alternative role titles, and keeping opportunities moving through saved searches and alerts. Update speeds vary, so treat each listing as an entry point rather than the only source of truth.

## Common Mainland China entry points

- [BOSS Zhipin](https://www.zhipin.com/) covers a broad range of roles and includes campus and overseas-graduate entry points.
- [51job](https://www.51job.com/) and [Zhaopin](https://www.zhaopin.com/) are general recruitment platforms useful for comparing role titles across cities and industries.
- [Shixiseng](https://www.shixiseng.com/) focuses on internships and can help students looking for an early work experience.
- [Nowcoder Jobs](https://www.nowcoder.com/jobs/) concentrates on campus and technology recruitment alongside assessment and interview communities.

## Common Hong Kong entry points

- [Jobsdb Hong Kong](https://hk.jobsdb.com/) offers broad role categories, saved searches, company discovery, and application history.
- [CTgoodjobs](https://www.ctgoodjobs.hk/) and [cpjobs](https://www.cpjobs.com/hk/en) provide additional Hong Kong listings, company information, and career resources.
- Use the [Hong Kong Civil Service Bureau recruitment page](https://www.csb.gov.hk/english/recruit/7.html) as the official entry point for civil-service roles.
- University career portals, student emails, and graduate recruitment events may carry student- or alumni-only opportunities that do not appear on public boards.

No board is a complete ranking of the market. Employers choose different channels, and the same opening may appear on several platforms.

## Search flexibly

Combine role, skill, industry, and location terms, including synonymous job titles. A few useful saved searches are calmer than restarting from zero every day.

## Verify before investing time

Check the employer site for responsibilities, location, candidate eligibility, deadlines, and application status. Old listings, third-party copies, and duplicate posts may not update together. When a board conflicts with the company careers page or direct recruiter communication, use the official source.

For a promising role, record the company, title, link, discovery date, deadline, and status in your tracker. The board helps you find an entrance; the real JD, company information, and your own direction determine whether to walk through it.
