# Finding and Screening Suitable Roles

Use several sources: company career pages, university platforms, professional networks, specialist boards, and job alerts. Search related titles as well as your first-choice title; similar work is often labelled differently.

Before saving a role, check the work itself, location, application window, eligibility, language, team, and evidence requested. Then ask whether it fits what you are willing to do, what you can already demonstrate, and where you want to grow.

A lightweight tracker can record the link, deadline, status, next action, and questions to research. The goal is not to build a perfect database. It is to prevent promising opportunities and important follow-ups from disappearing in a pile of tabs.
