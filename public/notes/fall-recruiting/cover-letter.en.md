# Cover Letters

A cover letter is useful when it adds information the resume cannot: why this role, why this organisation, and why your experience makes the connection credible.

Open with the role and your strongest reason for applying. Use the middle to connect one or two relevant experiences to the employer's current needs. Close with interest in the next conversation. Specificity is more persuasive than repeating the company's values word for word.

Keep a reusable evidence bank, but tailor the argument. Check the company name, role title, contact name, and regional spelling before submitting. If a letter is optional and you have nothing meaningful to add, invest that time where it will improve the application more.
