# Referrals

A referral may help an application receive attention or give recruiters a trusted piece of context. It is not an offer guarantee and does not replace role fit or the formal process.

## When to ask

Ask when you understand the role, your materials are ready, and the person knows enough about you to make an informed choice. An immediate request from a stranger can ask them to take a reputational risk without evidence.

## Make the request easy to assess

Share the role, your reason for applying, the deadline, and a concise resume or link. Make it comfortable to decline; every employer has different rules and conflicts to consider.

## After the referral

Complete the application and interviews with the same care. Thank the person without repeatedly asking for internal updates. The relationship is more valuable than a single application link.
