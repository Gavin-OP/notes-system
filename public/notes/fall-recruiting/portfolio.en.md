# Portfolio

A portfolio gives space to work that cannot fit on a one-page resume: research, dashboards, design decisions, writing, code, experiments, or a detailed project process.

For each project, explain the context, your role, the problem, the process, the result, and what you would improve. Show enough evidence to make the work understandable without exposing confidential material. A small set of well-explained projects is stronger than a large gallery with no context.

Make the most relevant project easy to find, test every link on mobile and desktop, and provide a PDF alternative when appropriate. The portfolio should reduce questions about your contribution, not create new ones about navigation.
