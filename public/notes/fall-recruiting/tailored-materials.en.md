# Tailored Resume and Cover Letter

Tailoring does not mean rewriting everything for every role. It means helping the most relevant evidence appear sooner while keeping the facts stable.

## What to change in the resume

Prioritise experience closest to the responsibilities, use language the reader recognises, and retain enough context. Keywords help, but natural, accurate, defensible evidence matters more.

## What the cover letter should add

When requested—or when your motivation needs useful context—connect the employer, role, and your evidence briefly. The letter should add to the resume rather than repeat it in paragraphs.
