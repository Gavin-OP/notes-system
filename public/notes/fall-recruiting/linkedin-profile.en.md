# LinkedIn

LinkedIn can work as a searchable version of your professional story and as a low-pressure way to learn from people in roles you are exploring.

Use a clear headline, a short About section, accurate dates, and experience descriptions that agree with your resume. Add relevant projects, skills, education, and a professional photo if you are comfortable doing so. A customised URL makes the profile easier to share.

Search with several versions of a role title and follow teams or companies you care about. When connecting, a brief message with real context is more useful than a generic request. After a conversation, send thanks and keep notes on what you learned; networking is a relationship, not a one-time request for a referral.

Check privacy and notification settings before making many edits. Your profile should be discoverable without revealing information you would rather keep private.
