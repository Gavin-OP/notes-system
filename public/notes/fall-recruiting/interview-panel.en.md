# Panel Interview

A panel interview brings several interviewers into the same conversation. They may represent business, technical, team, and recruitment perspectives. Your job is not to please everyone simultaneously; it is to make each answer clear to the room.

If names and roles are provided, understand how each person relates to the job. Begin an answer with the person who asked, then include the rest of the panel through natural eye contact. Online, look at the camera sometimes rather than monitoring your own image.

Different interviewers may challenge opposite sides of an answer. Clarify the current assumption and explain when a changed priority would change your choice. Updating a view in response to new information is not inconsistency.

Afterwards, record who asked what, how the team described the work, and what remains uncertain. Your questions can be directed by expertise: business goals to a leader, collaboration to a future colleague, and process to HR.
